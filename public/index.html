    How to Play Pablo

    Goal

    Have the lowest hand value each round. First player to reach the score limit loses.

    Your Turn

    Draw from deck → swap it with a hand card, or discard it. Special cards trigger their ability when discarded.

    Snapping

    Any time — if a card in your hand matches the top discard rank, snap it to remove it. Wrong snap = penalty card.
    Desktop: press 1–4  |  Mobile: swipe the card

    Calling Pablo

    On your turn, call Pablo if you think you have the lowest hand. Everyone else gets one more turn. Wrong call = +10 pts penalty.

    Setup

    Each player is dealt 4 cards. At the start, cards at positions 1 and 4 are briefly revealed for 3 seconds — memorise them. Positions 2 and 3 stay hidden.

    Taking a Turn

    • Draw the top card from the deck.
    • Either swap it with one of your hand cards (the old card goes to discard) or discard it directly.
    • If you discard a special card (7, 8, 9, or 10) you must use its ability instead of ending your turn immediately.

    Special Abilities

    • 7 / 8 — Peek: Choose one of your own cards to reveal for 3 seconds.
    • 9 / 10 — Spy: Choose any one of an opponent's cards to reveal for 3 seconds.
    • You may skip a special ability if you don't want to use it.

    Snapping

    At any point in the game (even on someone else's turn), you can snap one of your own cards if its rank matches the current top discard card.

    • Success: The card is discarded and removed from your hand.
    • Failure: You draw a penalty card from the deck, added to your hand.

    Calling Pablo

    On your turn, before drawing, you can call "Pablo" if you believe your hand is the lowest. This ends your turn and triggers the last round — every other player gets exactly one more turn.

    • If you have the lowest hand: you win the round with 0 points.
    • If someone else ties or beats you: you pay your hand total plus a 10-point penalty.

    Scoring a Round

    Count the total value of all cards in your hand. The player with the lowest total adds 0 points. Everyone else adds their hand total to their cumulative score.

    Winning the Game

    Play continues over multiple rounds. The first player to reach or exceed the score limit is eliminated — everyone else wins.

    Deck Running Out

    If the deck empties, all discards except the top card are reshuffled into a new deck.

    🧠Memorise your starting cards (positions 1 & 4). You see them once — track them mentally throughout the round.
    👀When you swap a card in, you see it briefly. Use that window to decide whether it's worth keeping.
    ♥Red Kings (♥♦) are worth −1 point — the best card in the game. Never swap them out unless you have no choice.
    🃏Black Kings (13 pts), Queens (12), and Jacks (11) are your biggest liabilities. Prioritise replacing them early.
    ⚡Snap aggressively. A wrong snap is just one penalty card — the upside of removing a high card from your hand is usually worth the risk.
    🔍Use Spy (9/10) before calling Pablo to confirm an opponent has high cards — it makes your Pablo call much safer.
    ⏳Don't call Pablo too early. Everyone gets one more turn after you do — a player sitting on a low hand will beat you.
    🎯Watch the discard pile rank. If you know a card in your hand matches, be ready to snap the instant it appears.
